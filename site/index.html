RSA Challenge

Factoring / decryption challenges in the spirit of the RSA Factoring Challenge. Each challenge publishes a modulus N, e = 65537 and a ciphertext c (textbook RSA, no padding, of a UTF-8 message read as a big-endian integer). Solve it by submitting the factors p, q, the private exponent d, or the plaintext.

Self-check only: verification runs entirely in your browser against SHA-256 commitments — nothing is sent anywhere and there is no leaderboard.
